# AI Prompt Library — Resume and LinkedIn Portfolio Kit

Use only your own verified facts. Never ask AI to invent experience or metrics.

## Prompt 1 — Research summary
“Summarise current ATS-friendly resume principles for CSE freshers. Separate evidence-based guidance from general suggestions. Use reliable university career-service and official platform sources. Include source links and limitations.”

## Prompt 2 — Resume drafting
“Create a one-page, plain-text resume for a CSE student using only the facts below. Do not invent dates, CGPA, skills, achievements or metrics. Mark missing details as [PLACEHOLDER]. Target role: [ROLE]. Facts: [PASTE VERIFIED FACTS].”

## Prompt 3 — Job description keyword comparison
“Compare my resume with this job description. Return (1) relevant keywords already supported by my experience, (2) missing skills I should not claim, and (3) truthful edits. Do not add unsupported claims. Resume: [TEXT]. Job description: [TEXT].”

## Prompt 4 — LinkedIn headline
“Suggest three concise LinkedIn headlines for a CSE student. Use only these verified skills and interests: [FACTS]. Avoid seniority claims and exaggerated words.”

## Prompt 5 — LinkedIn About
“Edit this About section for clarity and grammar while preserving my meaning and student status. Do not add achievements or skills. Text: [TEXT].”

## Prompt 6 — Cover letter
“Draft a concise cover letter for this internship using only my resume and the job description. Do not invent company knowledge, dates or achievements. Mark missing details as placeholders.”

## Prompt 7 — Proofreading
“Proofread this resume for grammar, spelling, consistency and readability. Do not change factual meaning. List every suggested factual change separately for my approval.”

## Prompt 8 — Verification
“Check each factual claim in this draft against the source facts below. Create a table with claim, evidence, unsupported/needs confirmation, and recommended correction. Do not infer missing facts.”

## Prompt 9 — Portfolio page
“Organise my verified student profile into About, Skills, Education, Internship, Projects and Contact sections. Mark missing details as placeholders and do not publish private information.”

## Prompt 10 — Presentation
“Create a five-slide self-introduction outline for a CSE student: introduction, education and skills, internship learning, projects, goals and contact. Keep it factual and beginner-friendly.”

## Prompt 11 — Two-tool comparison
“Review the same resume draft. Identify unclear wording, ATS readability risks and unsupported claims. Do not rewrite personal facts. Explain uncertainty.”

## Prompt 12 — Responsible AI review
“Review this AI-generated career content for hallucinations, privacy risks, outdated advice and exaggerated claims. Separate confirmed issues from items that need human checking.”
