# Research Summary — ATS Resume and LinkedIn Profile for CSE Freshers

## 1. Audience and purpose
Target audience: final-year CSE students and recent graduates / freshers. The kit is also editable for a current CSE student preparing an early portfolio. The purpose is to present real education, skills, project work and learning experience clearly without exaggeration.

## 2. ATS-friendly resume principles
Research across university career-service guidance suggests:
- Use a simple, readable, consistent layout.
- Prefer standard section headings such as Education, Skills, Projects and Experience.
- Tailor the content to the role using accurate terms from the job description.
- Use specific, active, fact-based bullet points.
- Avoid unnecessary graphics, tables, text boxes and decorative layouts where parsing may be unreliable.
- Check spelling, dates, contact information and PDF conversion.
- A one-page resume is usually suitable for an undergraduate / fresher, unless an employer requests another format.
- Never add a skill, certification, metric or experience that cannot be verified.

Important limitation: no resume format can guarantee an ATS score or interview. Different employers use different systems and settings.

## 3. LinkedIn profile principles
LinkedIn's official help guidance describes the introduction area as the first part visitors see and identifies the headline as a way to communicate an area of expertise or professional status. Its About section is intended to express a person's mission, motivation and skills. Therefore:
- Use a clear headline that identifies student status and genuine focus areas.
- Write a concise About section in the first person, with authentic details.
- Add education, experience, skills and projects accurately.
- Keep profile links and contact information current.
- Review privacy before publishing.

## 4. Role-based resume versions
1. Software / web development: prioritise programming, web skills, software projects and development internship.
2. Data / AI: include only verified data, Python, statistics or AI coursework and projects. Do not claim unearned proficiency.
3. Technical support: highlight communication, teamwork and any real troubleshooting practice; add technical support tools only after learning them.

## 5. AI-assisted workflow
Use AI for brainstorming, editing, keyword comparison and proofreading—not for inventing personal facts. The student remains responsible for checking every claim. Compare outputs from at least two AI tools where available, and record disagreements or unsupported suggestions.

## 6. Ethical and privacy rules
- Do not invent skills, marks, job experience, certifications or project outcomes.
- Do not paste passwords, private identifiers or sensitive records into AI tools.
- Use placeholders for personal contact information in demo copies.
- Obtain permission before sharing another person's details or project materials.
- Check the final document manually.
